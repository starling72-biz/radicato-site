Nourishing Boone

We're just a couple locals, sourcing locally.

Radicáto is two great friends working each day to make the local community we love a little better — one dish at a time. We cook fresh, use real ingredients, and source many of them locally from farms and suppliers right here in Iowa.

Since 2022, we've been cooking out of the basement of Boone First United Methodist Church — and we're proud of the home it's given us. One day soon, we'll be cooking from our very own space in downtown Boone, the Radicáto Kitchen. Until then, we're thrilled to be your go-to for deliciously easy meal prep done the Radicáto way.
